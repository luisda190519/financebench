# Ethics Statement

FinanceBench is designed to evaluate the computational capabilities of large language models on financial analysis tasks. It is intended for research and model development purposes and does not constitute financial advice. We emphasize that LLM-generated financial projections should never serve as the sole basis for investment decisions, loan applications, or business planning; human financial expertise remains essential for high-stakes decisions (Financial Stability Board, 2024; CFA Institute, 2025).

All 1,000 test cases are synthetically generated using seeded pseudo-random number generators. No real company data, personal information, or proprietary financial records are used. Business names, parameters, and scenarios are fictional, though designed to be realistic. The dataset can be freely shared and reproduced without privacy concerns.

We release the complete benchmark as open source—including the dataset, generation code, oracle engine, comparison infrastructure, prompt templates, and evaluation scripts—enabling independent verification, reproducibility, and community contribution. The authors have no commercial conflicts of interest related to this work.

By systematically identifying where and how LLMs fail at financial computation, we aim to improve the safety and reliability of AI-assisted financial analysis. Establishing rigorous baselines enables the research community to measure progress and helps developers build tools that are more transparent about their limitations—a prerequisite for responsible deployment in financial services.
