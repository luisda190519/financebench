# Section 10: Conclusion — Plan

## Objective
Synthesis and forward-looking closure. Must leave readers with a clear understanding of what was achieved, what was learned, and why it matters.

## Word Count Target
250–350 words

## Structure (4 paragraphs)

---

### Paragraph 1: Recapitulation (60–80 words)

- What we did: "We presented FinanceBench, a benchmark of 1,000 deterministic test cases with a ground-truth computational oracle for evaluating LLMs on structured financial analysis tasks."
- What it covers: 10 task categories, 10 business types, 4 economic scenarios, 3 difficulty levels
- How it works: field-by-field comparison with three-tier tolerance classification across 316 individual data points per case

### Paragraph 2: Key Findings (80–100 words)

- 2–3 headline results WITH specific numbers:
  1. "[Best model] achieves X% overall accuracy, with Y% on direct arithmetic but only Z% on multi-step cascading computations"
  2. "The proprietary-open source gap averages X pp but narrows to Y pp on simpler tasks"
  3. "IRR calculation and tax loss carryforward emerge as universal failure modes across all evaluated models"
- What this reveals: current LLMs have partial but incomplete financial reasoning capability

### Paragraph 3: Impact (60–80 words)

- For the research community: first standardized evaluation framework enabling reproducible comparison of financial LLM capabilities
- For practitioners: evidence-based guidance on model selection and limitation awareness
- For the financial AI ecosystem: identifies specific capability gaps that fine-tuning, tool augmentation, or architectural innovation could address
- Open-source release enables community extension and competition

### Paragraph 4: Vision (50–70 words)

- "As LLMs are increasingly deployed for financial decision support — from startup viability assessment to investment analysis — rigorous evaluation becomes a necessity rather than a luxury."
- FinanceBench is a step toward ensuring AI-assisted financial analysis is trustworthy, transparent, and improvement-oriented
- Call to action: use, extend, and improve the benchmark

---

## Rules
- **NO** new ideas or arguments not previously discussed
- **NO** new references
- **NO** technical details not already presented
- DO include specific numbers (this is what readers remember)
- DO end on a forward-looking note

## Writing Notes
- Write second-to-last (before Abstract)
- Should feel like a satisfying wrap-up, not a rushed ending
- Mirror the Introduction's structure in reverse: specific → general → vision
- The final sentence should be memorable
